## Cascade Unified School District — Letter of Support

> [Cascade Unified School District Letterhead]
>
> September 2026
>
> EPA Region 10 Environmental Education Local Grants Program Review Panel
>
> Dear Review Panel,
>
> Cascade Unified School District is pleased to confirm our support for
> Cascade Watershed Alliance's proposed Youth Stream Monitoring Program
> and to authorize the participation of three of our schools — Riverside
> Middle School, Fernwood High School, and Willow Creek Elementary — for
> the full award period.
>
> Our district has partnered informally with Cascade Watershed Alliance
> for the past two years on smaller classroom water-quality units, and we
> have seen firsthand the effect hands-on monitoring has on student
> engagement with science coursework. Local water quality is a documented
> concern in our community: the district's own environmental science
> curriculum review (2025) identified watershed literacy as a priority
> gap area across our science standards, which this program would
> directly help close.
>
> We confirm that all three participating schools have agreed to
> integrate the program's curriculum into existing science coursework for
> the award period, and that our facilities are available for the
> classroom sessions and after-school monitoring club meetings this
> program requires.
>
> Sincerely,
>
> Dr. Elena Vasquez, Assistant Superintendent for Curriculum
> Cascade Unified School District

## Fernwood High School Green Team — Letter of Commitment

>
> EPA Region 10 Environmental Education Local Grants Program Review Panel
>
> Dear Review Panel,
>
> Fernwood High School commits to the following in support of Cascade
> Watershed Alliance's Youth Stream Monitoring Program, contingent on the
> program's receipt of EPA funding and the associated $5,000 subaward for
> our rain garden demonstration project:
>
> Sarah Mercer, our Green Team faculty advisor and AP Environmental
> Science teacher, will commit 4 hours per week for the full 12-month
> award period to lead the rain garden project — coordinating student
> volunteers, managing subaward funds for materials and site preparation,
> and reporting project progress to Cascade Watershed Alliance in line
> with the subaward's reporting terms. This time commitment is in
> addition to, not a reallocation of, Ms. Mercer's existing instructional
> duties, and has been approved by Fernwood's principal as part of her
> extracurricular advisory role.
>
> We understand this commitment is specific to the award period stated
> above and will notify Cascade Watershed Alliance promptly of any change
> in Ms. Mercer's availability that would affect this project.
>
> Sincerely,
>
> Sarah Mercer, Green Team Faculty Advisor
> Fernwood High School

>

>
> Cascade Land Trust commits the following resources to Cascade Watershed

>
