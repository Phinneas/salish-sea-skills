## 1. Basic Information
- **Agency:** U.S. EPA, Region 10
- **Funding available:** Up to $300,000 for Region 10 (part of a $3M nationwide program)
- **Award range:** $50,000–$100,000 in federal funds per award; 3–4 awards expected
- **Project period:** 1–2 years, starting no earlier than August 1, 2020
- **Geographic scope:** Alaska, Idaho, Oregon, Washington only
- **Deadline:** January 6, 2020, 11:59 PM ET

## 2. Eligibility
**Eligible applicants (verbatim categories):**
- Local education agencies
- Colleges and universities
- State education or environmental agencies
- 501(c)(3) nonprofit organizations
- Noncommercial educational broadcasting entities (FCC-licensed)
- Tribal education agencies meeting specific criteria

**Exclusions:** For-profit organizations; 501(c)(4) organizations that engage in lobbying.

**Geographic eligibility:** Applicant organizations must be located in the US/territories, with a majority of project activities in the US, US–Canada, or US–Mexico. Project activities specifically must be located in Region 10 (AK/ID/OR/WA).

**Cost share:** Minimum 25% of total project cost in non-federal funds. Federal funds cannot count toward this match.

**Subaward requirement:** Exactly 25% (no more, no less) of EPA funds awarded must be distributed as subawards of $5,000 or less each. Example given in source: a $100,000 EPA award requires exactly $25,000 in subawards of ≤$5,000 each. This is a separate mechanical requirement from the cost-share match.

**Submission limits:** Not specified in the excerpted text.

## 3. Program Description
**Purpose:** Fund environmental education projects that design, demonstrate, and/or disseminate environmental education practices, methods, or techniques, addressing designated educational and environmental priorities.

**Required priorities (must address ≥1 from each list):**
- *Educational priorities:* Agricultural Education, Community Projects, Career Development
- *Environmental priorities (must name a specific topic):* Air quality improvement; clean and safe water; chemical safety (integrated pest management, vegetation management); land revitalization and contamination prevention

**Required outcomes/performance measures:** Increase in environmental and/or conservation literacy; encouragement of behavior that leads to environmental/conservation stewardship. No numeric performance targets specified in the excerpted text.

## 4. Application Contents and Format
- Work Plan: maximum 8 single-spaced pages total (Project Summary ~1 page recommended, Detailed Project Description, Project Evaluation)
- Required, no page limit: Detailed Budget Table and Narrative, Timeline, Logic Model, Programmatic Capability and Past Performance narrative, Partnership Letters of Commitment (if claiming partnership points)
- Format: letter-sized (8.5×11"), ≥10-point font, ≥1-inch margins, single-spaced
- Submission: electronic via Grants.gov (limited exceptions for lack of internet access)

## 6. Application Review Information

### Threshold / responsiveness criteria (go/no-go — screened before scoring)
1. Application does not substantially comply with submission instructions/requirements
2. Application submitted after the deadline (unless EPA system failure or extreme circumstances proven)
3. Applicant is not an eligible organization per the Eligibility section
4. Applicant cannot demonstrate the 25% non-federal match
5. Requested amount falls outside the $50,000–$100,000 federal range
6. Applicant cannot describe exactly 25% of EPA funds going to subawards of $5,000 or less
7. Project does not satisfy the RFA's definition of environmental education
8. Application does not address at least one educational priority
9. Application does not address at least one environmental priority with a named specific topic
10. Applicant previously received an EE grant for this project and cannot demonstrate expansion/enhancement of it
11. Proposed activities are not located within Region 10 (AK/ID/OR/WA)
