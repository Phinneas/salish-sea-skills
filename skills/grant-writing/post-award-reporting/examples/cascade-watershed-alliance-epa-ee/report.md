### Narrative summary
The program is on track on every output tied to direct program delivery —
training, monitoring, and restoration workdays are all proceeding on the
planned cadence, with Willow Creek's later training date reflecting that
school's own academic calendar rather than a program delay.

The one output behind plan is the Riverside Middle School subaward: the
Environmental Club's site plan for the native plant buffer project
required an additional round of review by the district's facilities
office before subaward execution, which pushed finalization past the
original fall target. The subaward has not yet been disbursed. The
Program Coordinator met with Riverside's site coordinator in January to
finalize the site plan and expects to execute the subaward by early
March 2021 — this will be confirmed in the next report. This delay does
not affect the program's ability to meet its overall subaward requirement
within the award period, but it is reported here rather than held until
resolved, consistent with reporting progress as it actually stands.
