# Budget Narrative: Cascade Watershed Alliance Youth Stream Monitoring Program

**Prepared:** 2026-08-28 · **For opportunity:** Environmental Education Local Grants Program (EPA Region 10)
**Total project cost:** $80,701 · **Amount requested:** $60,000 · **Cost share:** $20,701 ($16,001 cash + $4,700 in-kind)

## Indirect cost rate
**Basis:** De minimis rate, elected at 15% of Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC). Cascade Watershed Alliance has no negotiated indirect cost rate agreement (NICRA) and has not previously elected the de minimis rate on a federal award, so this election is available under 2 CFR 200.414(f). Once elected, the org will apply this rate consistently across all its federal awards until it either negotiates a real rate or elects to negotiate one.
**MTDC calculation:** $66,420 total direct charges − $2,550 (Equipment, excluded from MTDC per 2 CFR 200.1) = **$63,870 MTDC**
**Indirect costs:** $63,870 × 15% = **$9,581**

## Budget narrative by category

### Personnel — $33,300
- **Program Coordinator** (0.35 FTE, $54,000 annual salary) — $18,900. Oversees the full program: manages the 3 subawards to partner organizations, coordinates the 4 quarterly restoration workdays, and leads the year-end community data showcase. This is the position accountable for the two mandatory 25% mechanics (match and subaward structuring) the NOFO requires.
- **Education Coordinator** (0.30 FTE, $48,000 annual salary) — $14,400. Delivers the classroom curriculum at all 3 partner schools, trains students in monitoring protocols, and administers the pre/post water-quality literacy assessment.

### Fringe Benefits — $7,992
24% of salaries, the org's standard fringe rate (covers payroll taxes, a modest health stipend, and retirement contribution match for part-time staff at this FTE level).

### Travel — $1,350
Staff mileage reimbursement (IRS standard rate) for site visits to the 3 partner schools and monitoring locations: 12 monthly monitoring trips, 4 restoration workdays, and periodic school curriculum-delivery visits, for both the Program Coordinator and Education Coordinator across the award period.

### Equipment — $2,550
Three multi-parameter water-quality test kits (pH, turbidity, temperature, dissolved oxygen) at $850 each, needed to equip monitoring at all 3 school-linked sites simultaneously rather than staff rotating a single shared kit, which the org's current one-kit setup does not support at this program scale. Excluded from the MTDC base per 2 CFR 200.1's equipment exclusion.

### Supplies — $3,900
- Curriculum materials and student field notebooks — $1,200
- Restoration workday supplies (native plants, hand tools, gloves) for 4 workdays — $1,800
- Monitoring consumables (reagents, calibration solutions) for the program year — $900

All tied directly to the monitoring, curriculum, and restoration activities in the logic model.

### Contractual — $15,000
Three subawards of $5,000 each, structured to meet the NOFO's mandatory
requirement that exactly 25% of EPA funds ($60,000 × 25% = $15,000) be
distributed as subawards of $5,000 or less:
- **Riverside Middle School Environmental Club** — $5,000 for a native
  plant riparian buffer installation at the school's adjacent monitoring
  site, executed by students under the Education Coordinator's oversight.
- **Fernwood High School Green Team** — $5,000 for a rain garden
  demonstration project addressing stormwater runoff at the school site.
- **Cascade Land Trust** — $5,000 for a streambank stabilization
  micro-project at the confluence monitoring site, coordinated with the
  trust's existing field staff.

Each subaward is below the $25,000 MTDC-inclusion cap, so its full amount
is included in the MTDC base above.

### Construction — $0
Not applicable to this program.

### Other — $2,328
- Year-end community data showcase (venue rental, printing, refreshments) — $1,100
- Annual subscription to a low-cost water-quality data logging/reporting platform — $720
- Outreach printing and materials for the showcase and ongoing community updates — $508

## Cost share / match
**Required:** minimum 25% of total project cost in non-federal funds (2 CFR 200.306 basis, as stated in the NOFO). At a total project cost of $80,701, the required match is $20,175.25.

**Provided:** $20,701, comfortably clearing the requirement with roughly $526 of margin — deliberately not sized to exactly the minimum, per the fit assessment's flag that a zero-margin match at a higher award level was a real risk.
- **Cash match — $16,001:** from the org's unrestricted operating reserve, previously approved by the board for this program's match obligation.
- **In-kind match — $4,700:** partner land trust's donated field-site access and equipment-sharing (valued at the trust's standard in-kind rate, $2,700) and 3 partner schools' donated classroom space for curriculum delivery (valued at $2,000 based on the district's standard facility-use rate for community partners).
