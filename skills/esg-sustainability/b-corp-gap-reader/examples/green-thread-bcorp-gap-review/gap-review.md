# Example: Green Thread Studio B Corp gap review

## Organization profile and boundary

Green Thread Studio is a fictional 11-person apparel-repair cooperative in Oregon. This indicative review covers the operating entity, 2025 practices, and primary suppliers. The live B Impact Assessment track and version were not supplied; therefore no official score or certification conclusion is estimated.

## Executive gap summary

The strongest evidence is the repair model and a basic wage record. The largest readiness gaps are formal worker voice, documented governance accountability, supplier screening, environmental measurement, and customer-data controls. The organization should improve evidence quality before attempting to infer points.

## Evidence request list

Request the current legal/accountability documents, worker handbook or policies, wage and hours dataset, grievance and incident logs, supplier register and screening record, chemical inventory and waste manifests, energy data, customer privacy/retention rule, accessibility review, and a documented impact-model measurement method.

## Indicative readiness narrative

Green Thread shows a credible purpose-led operating model but is not yet evidence-ready for a reliable certification assessment. The organization should close high-priority management-system and measurement gaps, confirm its current B Lab track, and complete the live B Impact Assessment before discussing a score or certification pathway.

## Do not claim

Do not claim B Corp certification, a passing score, official B Lab alignment, legal compliance, verified environmental savings, or complete stakeholder impact based on this review.
