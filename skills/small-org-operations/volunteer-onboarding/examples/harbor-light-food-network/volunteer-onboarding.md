# Harbor Light Food Network — Pantry Volunteer Onboarding

**Role:** Saturday distribution greeter and stock assistant  
**Supervisor:** Saturday Site Lead  
**Status:** Ready for pilot review

## Role card

The volunteer welcomes guests, explains the queue, restocks labeled shelves, and escalates access, safety, or safeguarding concerns to the Site Lead. The role does not include eligibility decisions, handling cash, transporting guests, photographing guests, or providing medical advice.

## Intake and screening

Collect name, contact, availability, language/access needs, relevant experience, emergency contact, and communication consent. Because the role serves the public but does not involve unsupervised work with minors, use the organization’s standard reference and conduct screening; escalate any role change to the safeguarding owner before placement.

## Orientation agenda

| Module | Demonstration or acknowledgment |
|---|---|
| Mission and dignity | volunteer states how to offer choice and respect |
| Boundaries and confidentiality | signs conduct acknowledgment |
| Food and site safety | identifies handwashing, lifting, and spill procedure |
| Escalation | role-plays a guest complaint and safeguarding concern |
| Attendance and communication | confirms cancellation route and supervisor |

## First-shift readiness check

The Site Lead observes the volunteer greeting a guest, locating three stock categories, and escalating a simulated concern. Access to the volunteer tablet is granted only after all three demonstrations are complete.

## First 30 days

The Site Lead conducts a five-minute check-in after shift one, a pulse check during week two, and a day-30 review. Record training date, result, accommodations, concerns, recognition, and next placement decision. On pause or exit, remove tablet access and retain only necessary records.
