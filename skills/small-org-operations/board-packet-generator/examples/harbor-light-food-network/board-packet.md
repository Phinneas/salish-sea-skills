## Decision brief: Saturday site pilot

**Recommendation:** Approve a six-month pilot at the Eastside Community Center, capped at $15,000, with a 60-day review.  
**Alternatives:** defer; run a four-month pilot; seek a partner before launch.  
**Evidence:** expansion memo; August finance dashboard; volunteer capacity note.  
**Risks:** volunteer shortfall, food-supply variability, reserve draw.  
**Mitigations:** launch only after minimum staffing threshold; weekly supply check; stop-loss review at day 60.  
**Owner:** Executive Director.  
**DRAFT MOTION:** “I move that the board authorize a six-month Eastside Saturday distribution pilot, with spending not to exceed $15,000 from unrestricted reserves, subject to a 60-day board review.”
