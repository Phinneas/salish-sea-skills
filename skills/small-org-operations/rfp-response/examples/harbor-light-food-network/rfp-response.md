## Executive summary

Harbor Light proposes a hub-based meal-access service that uses existing pantry routes, trained volunteers, and monthly participation reporting. The approach is designed to reduce missed pickups by placing predictable service at three county hubs and escalating access barriers to the County project manager.

## Approach

During mobilization, the Programs Director will confirm hub schedules, accessibility, referral partners, and escalation contacts. Each service day will use a site checklist, two-person cashless handling controls, attendance count, incident log, and closeout report. The County receives a monthly dashboard with distributions, attendance, cancellations, barriers, and corrective actions.

## Budget assumptions

Pricing is pending CFO confirmation. The draft assumes three hubs, one service day per week, existing vehicles, and County-provided site access. It excludes vehicle purchase, unplanned emergency distributions, and scope changes not approved through written change control.
